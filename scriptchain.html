ScriptChain Health | Agentic AI-powered Food and Exercise as Medicine Platform

Reducing metabolic syndrome through custom diet and exercise plans, all without Nutritionists or Exercise Physiologists

Job Overview

Worked as the lead product designer at ScriptChain Health to design all screens associated with both the patient and provider dashboards for their upcoming SAAS application.

Role

UX Research

UX/UI Design

Problem

The cost of a Nutritionist and Exercise Physiologists for patients with metabolic syndrome can be massive and primary care physicians don't have the capacity to monitor patient's diet and exercise.

Solution

Utilize trained agentic AI agents with EHR data to flag patients for metabolic syndrome and develop specialized diet and exercise plans for them to follow.
