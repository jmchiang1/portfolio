Neon Drift | Music Festival Concept

Project Overview

Ever since I could remember, I have loved chill pop music, and most recently lofi hip hop beats. But there aren't any music festivals that encompass this sub-genre of music, so I created a website for it and animated it!

Music inspiration came from artists like Keshi, Joji, SZA, and Giveon. While visual inspiration came from Vaporwave and Synthwave aesthetics.
