Interaction design 2026

Opening the learning path, the nodes draw in and the next lesson floats up above the nav. Tapping the list morphs it into a full lesson sheet: it widens to the edges, drops over the nav and staggers its rows in while the path dims.

SkillCat Course Added

Interaction design 2026

Adding a course earns a badge. It rises oversized and leaning back, flips once, then stamps down to size as the chevron lights up and sparks fly, with sound locked to the impact.
