Title screens

Five versions of a screen that plays before the home page. The previews run live. Open one to try it for real: each start plays its exit and lands on your actual home page.

1. Keycaps

Open full screen

Your name is the keyboard. Bit hops across the keys typing it, and the spacebar starts the site. Type a letter from the name and its key presses.

Exit: the keys fall away and the keyboard opening on Home takes over.

2. Main Menu

See four walls

A classic title layout: your name and the menu in the center, with your real work around it. Level Select lights up the highlighted project.

Exit: a dither fills the screen, then Home or the chosen case study opens.

3. Star Map

Open full screen

The calmest version and the closest to Megan's. Your case studies orbit as planets you can click, with a live New York clock in the corner.

Exit: the stars settle into Home's dot grid and the blue floor rises beneath them.

4. Attract Mode

Open full screen

An arcade loop on Home's own monitor: title, case study roster, then real product clips. Someone who never presses anything still sees the work.

Exit: a coin drops and the CRT switches off inside the frame Home opens in.

5. Rooftop

Open full screen

The classic title scene: you and Bit on a New York rooftop at night, with windows flicking on and off. The most game-like version, and the biggest jump from Home's look.

Exit: the city sinks and the glow drains away, leaving the night sky.
